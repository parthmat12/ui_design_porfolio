A modern, cinematic music player designed around a 3D spatial album carousel. Explore albums in depth, bring your favorite track into focus, and enjoy a floating glassmorphism player with dynamic artwork-driven lighting.

✨ Key Features
🎨 3D Album Carousel — layered album cards with realistic depth and perspective
🌌 Dynamic Ambient Background — background glow adapts to the active album
💎 Glassmorphism Player — premium translucent floating playback interface
🎵 Real Audio Playback — play, pause, next, previous and seek controls
🌀 Smooth 3D Transitions — cinematic card movement and depth animation
🖱️ Mouse Interaction — drag, hover and interactive album navigation
📱 Touch & Mobile Support — swipe through albums naturally
⌨️ Keyboard Controls — navigate and control playback quickly
🔀 Shuffle & Repeat — flexible listening modes
❤️ Favorites — save favorite tracks locally
🌗 Dark Cinematic UI — immersive low-light visual experience
⚡ Responsive Design — optimized for desktop, tablet and mobile
♿ Reduced Motion Support — accessibility-friendly animations
🧠 Design Concept

The interface combines:

3D Spatial UI + Cinematic Visuals + Glassmorphism + Ambient Lighting + Music Interaction

Instead of treating album artwork as a simple thumbnail, the active album becomes the center of the experience.
